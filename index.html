FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to ship a vehicle?

Price depends on distance, vehicle size, open vs. enclosed, and how soon you need pickup. Most cross-country sedans on an open carrier fall in the $900–$1,500 range; shorter regional moves are often under $700. Use the quote tool above for an exact, all-in price for your route.

How long will my shipment take?

Pickup is usually scheduled within 1–5 days of booking. Once loaded, carriers average 400–500 miles per day — so coast to coast typically takes 7–10 days, and regional moves 1–3 days.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier we assign carries cargo insurance that covers your vehicle from pickup to delivery. Your driver documents the car's condition on a bill of lading at both ends.

What's the difference between open and enclosed transport?

Open carriers are the standard two-level trucks you see on the highway — the most affordable option and perfectly safe for everyday vehicles. Enclosed trailers fully cover the vehicle and cost roughly 40–60% more; they're the right choice for luxury, exotic, classic, or low-clearance cars.

Do I have to pay a deposit up front?

A small deposit reserves your spot once a carrier is assigned. The balance is paid directly to the driver at delivery (cash, cashier's check, or card, depending on the carrier). No payment is due until your car is actually scheduled.

Can you ship a vehicle that doesn't run?

Yes. Non-running vehicles need a carrier with a winch, which adds a modest fee. Just select "not operable" in the quote tool and it's priced in automatically.

Can I leave personal items in the car?

Carriers allow up to about 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or below the window line, but these aren't covered by cargo insurance. Remove valuables, toll tags, and anything loose.
